Why I built it

The Aggie Venture Fund professor wanted to keep our alumni connected.

I built this as the solution, and to help grow Texas A&M’s venture capital and entrepreneurship community.

What it is: a private directory, board, and CRM for 200+ alumni and sponsors.

Built to A&M security standards: invitation-only accounts, two-step sign-in for staff, and hosting on Texas A&M servers.

01 · Find anyone

Directory

Any member can find an alum in their field or city who’s ready to help.

Start with a search: look anyone up by name, role, company, or city.

Find people in your industry: see every alum working in the field you want to break into, from venture capital to healthcare.

Looking for help? Filter for alumni who are open to a chat, mentoring, or hiring, so you know exactly who to ask.

02 · Your profile

Profiles

Your career, your story, and your aspirations, all on one page, with every detail’s privacy in your hands.

Your story at a glance: your headline, current role, and city, plus your full work history, résumé, and LinkedIn.

You choose who sees what: show your email, phone, and résumé to all members, just to recruiters, or keep them private.

Get found for new roles: mark yourself open to work, and trusted AVF industry partners and recruiters can find you.

03 · Asks & updates

The Board

Where members bring opportunities to the network and celebrate each other’s wins.

Post an ask: launching a startup? Ask for a co-founder, early hires, or advisors, and give fellow alumni a chance to join you.

Share an update: announce a new role, a product launch, or a funding round, and the whole network can cheer you on with likes and comments.

05 · Security & hosting

Security

Hosted inside Texas A&M and built to the security controls it requires.

Hosted on campus: Texas A&M’s Private Cloud, cleared for confidential data.

Locked down: TCP 443 (TLS) only at the campus firewall, encrypted nightly backups, and server access via NetID + Duo VPN.

Meets Texas A&M’s controls: invitation-only accounts, lockouts, and two-step codes for admins (AC-2, AC-7, IA-2).
